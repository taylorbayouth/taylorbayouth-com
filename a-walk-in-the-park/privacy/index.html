Privacy Policy: A Walk in the Park

Effective November 12, 2026

A Walk in the Park is made by Bayouth Ventures, LLC ("we"). This policy covers the A Walk in the Park app for iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it doesn't send anything about you or your device over the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (sound, music and similar options) in files on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It also checks a few device settings while you play, such as Reduce Motion and whether other audio is playing, so it can adjust. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

Crash reports

The game doesn't send crash or diagnostic data to us or to anyone else. If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone or iPad settings, Apple may send us crash reports for the game. Those reports come through Apple, follow Apple's privacy policy, and don't identify you. We use them only to fix bugs and don't combine them with any other data.

Third parties

We don't share, sell or rent data to anyone. The game contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. If that ever changes, any third party with access to user data will be required to protect it at least as well as this policy does, and we'll update this page first.

Keeping and deleting data

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app, which deletes it. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, the save files may be included in that backup, which we can't access. Crash reports from Apple are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem.

To stop sharing crash reports, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements and turn off Share With App Developers. Because we don't hold any personal data, there's nothing for us to delete, but you can email us with any request about your data and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

The game is made for all ages. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the new version ships.
