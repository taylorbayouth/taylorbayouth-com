Privacy Policy: A Walk in the Park

Effective October 23, 2026

A Walk in the Park is made by Bayouth Ventures, LLC.

What we don't collect

The game has no accounts, ads or tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Crash reports

The game doesn't send crash or diagnostic data to us or to anyone else. If you've turned on Share with App Developers in your iPhone or iPad settings, Apple may send us crash reports for the game. Those reports come through Apple, follow Apple's privacy policy, and don't identify you. We use them only to fix bugs.

Your progress stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in a file on your device and never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game is safe for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the new version ships.
